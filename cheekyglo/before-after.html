A large CheekyGlo order runs to $25,000. The cap on their old platform was CN¥50,000 a day, roughly $11,000. “And I sent that money out, and it’s processing for three days, four days, five days. You get nervous... So it was me potentially borrowing money from my relatives in China and using them to pay for my payments,” says Fu.
